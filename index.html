About Sahara

We are the Official Social Welfare Society of the IIT Madras BS Degree Program, dedicated to providing assistance to those in need and creating opportunities for willing hearts.

Our mission

To make a meaningful impact through hands-on offline volunteering in established NGOs, engaging with social activists, and innovative competitions.

Our vision

A student community that comes together for social welfare, helping those in need and creating opportunities for willing hearts.

Our values

Selflessness, leadership, effective social interaction and public speaking, built through service to society.

Events & Activities

A look at what we have done together.

Offline Activity

Huge Smiles, Little Hearts with RHA

21–22 Nov 2023. With the Robin Hood Army in Indore, we ran a painting competition for 30 underprivileged children. We gave every child stationery, and the winners got sweets.

Guest Session

Session with Robin Singh, Founder of Peepal Farm

16 Oct 2023. Robin Singh, a computer scientist turned animal welfare leader, answered our questions and gave practical advice on helping animals.

Guest Session

RAKSHAK: A Session with Major Sidram Solankar

14 Aug 2023. Our first event, with the Kavach club of Bandipur house. Ex-Army officer and NSG commando Major Solankar talked about life as a commando and answered questions on the military.
